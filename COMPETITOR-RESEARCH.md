## New data-backed features

- **Same-hour model comparison:** Open-Meteo point forecasts for ECMWF IFS 0.25°, GFS seamless and ICON seamless, retrieved on demand and cached for 15 minutes. Temperature, hourly precipitation and wind use the same valid timestamp. Model resolution, physics and publication cadence differ. Agreement is not an accuracy guarantee.
- **US warning map:** [NWS active alerts](https://www.weather.gov/documentation/services-web-api) with severity styling, source text and expiry. Some alerts have no geometry; they remain in the list. Selecting one can retrieve its official affected-zone geometry. Partial or failed zone retrieval is labeled; failure never becomes an all-clear.
- **Tropical tracking:** [NHC GIS documentation](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/gis/) and its [official map service](https://mapservices.weather.noaa.gov/tropical/rest/services/tropical/NHC_tropical_weather/MapServer). Storm slots are discovered from the service; active forecast points, tracks and cones are queried as GeoJSON. Coverage is Atlantic and eastern/central Pacific NHC products, not worldwide cyclone coverage. A cone is not the entire area of impacts.
- **Observed radar:** RainViewer past-frame metadata, dynamically loaded tiles, frame-age display, coverage mask and bounded playback. A new frame replaces the old only after its tiles load; missing tiles or source failure remain explicit. No nowcast or detected lightning is manufactured.

## Why this does not become a wall of features

Today is the summary. Weather tiles open detail sheets. Forecast splits hourly and daily information. Radar keeps its timeline and layer controls over the map. Outdoor planning has dedicated activity/duration controls. More holds preferences, installation and source/coverage information. Page content scrolls inside the app only where its information requires it; the header and navigation stay available.

Fahrenheit and dark appearance are the new release defaults. Users can still choose Celsius or a different theme afterward. Dynamic Island and Home indicator work here means correct web safe-area spacing; this release does not provide native iOS Live Activities inside the Dynamic Island.
