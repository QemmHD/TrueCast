## Decisions about weather data

The established personal-use stack remains: Open-Meteo regional best-match point forecasts; independent NWS observations, narratives and warnings in US coverage; NOAA MRMS history; NOAA HRRR via Iowa Environmental Mesonet for US future reflectivity; DWD spatial forecast fields; NOAA lightning density/satellite; CAMS air quality/pollen; NIFC incident reports and SPC outlook polygons.

The overhaul improves how source data is used and interpreted:

- Upcoming signals use complete future hourly points. A rain-outlook timestamp corresponds to its actual peak probability, and missing probabilities do not become a dry forecast.
- Outdoor windows require every hourly sample in the complete future duration to satisfy daylight, temperature, wind, gust, rain-probability, accumulation and thunderstorm criteria. Missing required values reject a candidate. The whole window must fit the 72-hour search range.
- Best fit and Calmest are transparent preference rankings. They are not probability, forecast skill or safety scores. Soonest retains chronological selection. Windows do not overlap.
- Model spread is computed only from available values at the same valid hour. The main regional forecast is retained independently.
- Map buffering reuses real decoded frames, bounds memory and requests only nearby frames for the current viewport. Displayed times change after a verified image load. Buffering does not create intermediate meteorological measurements or optical-flow storm predictions.
- Existing ten-second foreground checks retain cancellation, cache age, retry/backoff and quiet identical-response handling. Providers publish on their own schedules; a visible app cannot force new observations every ten seconds.

A defensible claim of better forecast accuracy would require archived, location-specific forecasts and independent observations over representative weather. No such numerical superiority claim is made here. Apple's native notifications/widgets and CARROT's licensed strike features are different service/platform capabilities from a GitHub Pages PWA.

## Boundaries retained

DWD precipitation imagery is a native six-hour accumulation product. HRRR US reflectivity has native 15-minute leads. Observed radar and lightning have their own source cadence and coverage. Public density does not expose exact lightning-strike locations. Fire distances refer to returned incident points, not fire edges. iOS may suspend background web apps; no closed-app monitoring or native Dynamic Island activity was added.
