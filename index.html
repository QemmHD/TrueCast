Data sources

Coverage, sources & privacy

Forecast and air quality are model estimates. Radar shows observations where available; gaps in coverage are not clear skies. Official warning maps cover the US. Tropical tracking covers NHC Atlantic and eastern/central Pacific products. Lightning density is a regional observed grid, not individual strike tracking. Future maps are model forecasts; minute-specific rain arrival is not included. Radar defaults to both history and forecast: a six-hour window each in US coverage. Elsewhere, history is limited by the available feed. Future reflectivity is model-simulated; smooth blending does not add source frames.

Weather requests send the selected coordinates to the providers. Favorites and preferences stay on this browser. The app shell and a dated forecast can work offline, but current maps and warnings need internet. No background notification service is provided.

Weather data with perspective.

Forecast: Open-Meteo · Air quality: CAMS via Open-Meteo · Radar: RainViewer · US radar / future reflectivity: NOAA MRMS / HRRR via Iowa Environmental Mesonet · Future maps: DWD · Lightning density / satellite: NOAA nowCOAST · Fire incidents: NIFC / WFIGS · Storm outlooks: NOAA / SPC · US alerts: NWS

Personal, noncommercial edition. Saved places and preferences stay in this browser. Coordinates are sent to weather providers when you request a forecast. Maps request imagery from OpenStreetMap, RainViewer, DWD, NOAA and Iowa Environmental Mesonet when opened. Air and pollen requests use CAMS via Open-Meteo. US fire reports use NIFC / WFIGS via ArcGIS, and storm outlook polygons come from NOAA / SPC.

Install TrueCast

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open TrueCast in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (or Menu, then Share).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen. Keep Open as Web App enabled if shown.
  4. Tap Add.

Android

In Chrome, open the menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

Computer

In Chrome or Edge, look for the install icon in the address bar or choose Install TrueCast from the browser menu. Other browsers can use TrueCast as a website.

Your browser decides which installation options are available. Home Screen installation is separate from an App Store or Google Play listing.

Layers & map

Dark areas in the coverage mask have no radar observations. Native radar resolution stops at zoom 7. Zooming closer enlarges the same data.

Distance rings are geographic references, not strike locations. Fire reports are incident points, not fire edges or risk ratings. SPC outlooks are forecast areas, separate from current warnings. Future model maps and NOAA lightning density are available from Maps. Density is not an individual lightning-strike location. Official tropical track cones represent center-track uncertainty, not the complete area of storm impacts.
