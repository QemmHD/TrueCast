TrueCast on your iPhone

  1. Open this site in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (or the menu, then Share).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen. If shown, keep Open as Web App enabled.
  4. Tap Add, then open TrueCast from your Home Screen.

The app shell and your last saved forecast can work offline after your first visit. Fresh forecasts, search, alerts and radar require an internet connection. Background weather notifications are not available in this edition.
